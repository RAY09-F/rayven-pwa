STEP 1 — THE HUB: "THE BIFROST"
======================================================================
This is the page people see first. It should stop them.

  · A full-screen fragment-shader light bridge running into depth fog, with a
    star field behind it and colour banding that shifts slowly along its
    length. On the 2D fallback tier: a layered gradient bridge with animated
    scanlines and drifting stars — still handsome, never a blank page.
  · Three realm portals floating over it — storm blue, emerald, gold — each a
    ring of particles around a slowly rotating solid (reuse the engine's
    shapes). They respond to the pointer with parallax, and to device tilt on
    the phone, but ONLY after a tap-to-allow (iOS requires the gesture; never
    prompt on load).
  · Hovering or tapping a portal spins it up, brightens its realm colour, and
    dims the other two. Clicking opens the hall at that persona.
  · A boot sequence on first visit: the bridge draws itself from the horizon
    outward, the three portals ignite in sequence, the title lands. Skippable
    with any tap or key, and remembered in localStorage so it plays once —
    this is Rayan's own page, that is fine. Under 4 seconds. Hard time
    backstop so a thrown frame can never leave the page half-drawn.
  · A live status strip along the bottom, polled every 30 seconds:
      GET /healthz?public=1   if it exists (a public shape returning only
                              gods awake, councillor count, extension online,
                              paper P&L)
      GET /council/status     if it exists
      GET /paper-trading/status
    Render as: "3 GODS AWAKE · 20 COUNCILLORS · BROWSER LINKED · PAPER P&L
    +1.4% · SIMULATED". Any route that 404s or fails hides its own segment
    silently — never an error, never a broken strip, never a spinner that
    stays forever. Paper numbers ALWAYS carry the word SIMULATED.
  · A low Bifrost hum from the engine's synth, starting only after the first
    tap, with a visible mute control.
  · NO HINT OF HELA. Not in the markup, not in a comment, not in a fetch, not
    in the status strip, not in the boot text.

Build it as public/fx/hub.js plus the smallest possible edits to the hub page
(script tag + init + the strip container). If the hub page is small enough to
read safely, you may restructure its body; if it is large, use the anchor
method.

STEP 2 — THE COUNCILS PAGE (team.html)
======================================================================
This page already has a hand-built software 3D renderer and a CONFIG block at
the top of its script. KEEP BOTH. Do not rewrite the renderer. Do not change
any councillor name, colour, role line, theme line, tool wedge, or the live
tag sources. Do not touch Odin's five ids.

Add, via public/fx/team.js and the smallest possible hooks:

  · COUNCIL IN SESSION. When a councillor is running — the page can tell from
    the live data it already polls — its gem ignites, a stream of light
    particles flows down its tether toward the god at the centre, and the
    rest of the room dims slightly. When it finishes, one expanding ring on
    that gem and the room comes back. If nothing in the current data says
    "running", drive this from any change in a councillor's last-run
    timestamp instead, and say so in your report.
  · A depth-of-field vignette: the outer ring of the room falls slightly out
    of focus (pre-rendered blur snapshot, NOT a live filter — Rule 9).
  · A slow idle camera drift — a few degrees of rotation and a breath of
    scale over 30 seconds, so the room is never dead still.
  · A night-watch palette after 21:00 local: cooler, dimmer, the gems keeping
    their colour but the room going to deep blue-black.
  · Pointer proximity already spins nodes up; add a soft light that follows
    the pointer across the floor.
  · The room switch (THOR · LOKI · ODIN) uses the engine's realm-shift wipe.

If team.html's renderer exposes nothing on `window` for fx/team.js to hook,
ship only the overlay effects (vignette, palette, dim, pointer light, wipe)
and say so plainly in your report — do not refactor the renderer to make the
rest possible.
