# Website + automation pilot — first customer kit

Status: prepared for review, not sent. No customer, agreement or earnings are established. The attached demonstration is fictional.

## The offer

A clearer enquiry journey, with a useful next step for each request.

I can help improve one page on your existing website and connect one repeatable intake workflow. We start by agreeing what visitors should be able to do and what your team should receive. You review a working preview before any live change.

The pilot includes:

- One agreed page or enquiry journey, using your existing platform where feasible.
- One intake workflow with one agreed destination, such as a reviewed task queue.
- Clear required fields, validation and an acknowledgement state.
- Sample-data checks for successful, incomplete and failed submissions, with a manual fallback.
- One revision round, a walkthrough and operating notes.

Additional pages, integrations, hosting subscriptions and ongoing maintenance are scoped separately. Price and delivery dates follow a review of the actual platform, access and workflow; the fictional example's price is not a quote for a customer.

## Show the demo in two minutes

Open the downloadable Alder & Line sample in a browser. It works offline.

1. Show the page at phone width: a clear service, one main enquiry action and a short form.
2. Choose shelving, one to three months, $1,500–$5,000, and the fictional local area. Show the design-review route.
3. Change timing to within two weeks. Show that availability needs human review rather than promising a booking.
4. Explain the boundary: these are simulated queues and acknowledgements. A real pilot would connect the customer's agreed destination and test delivery/failure handling.
5. Ask which step in their current enquiry process takes the most manual work.

Do not present the fictional company as a client, the sample budget as market pricing, or the preview as a live integration.

## A short discovery conversation

- What should a visitor be able to request from this page?
- Where does the request go today, and who handles it?
- What information is usually missing?
- Which step is repeated by hand?
- What website platform and destination system are already in use?
- Who can approve the scope and provide appropriate access?
- What observable result would make this pilot useful?

Record the answers before filling the proposal. Keep unknown platform/integration details as TBD. Quote only a delivery path that can be tested.
