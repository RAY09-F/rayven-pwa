## DESIGN CONTRACT — Bifrost Aperture, ASGARD's signature HUD

Create a recognizable visual and interaction grammar rather than a collection of borrowed dashboard decorations. The index is a dark spatial chamber containing a dominant volumetric persona. A broken architectural aperture frames the presence, while clean conversation and compact tools occupy deliberately protected areas. The aperture is a designed frame and state surface, not another generic ring stack.

Use three signature elements consistently:

1. **The aperture:** a few asymmetric, chamfered light segments and recessed structural surfaces establish ASGARD's frame. Their geometry echoes selected persona features and remains recognizable without text.
2. **The seam:** one restrained line of light joins persona state, composer focus and the active result surface. It responds only to actual state; it never snakes across readable paragraphs.
3. **The receipt:** completed tool actions resolve into compact, verifiable result surfaces, with source, time and next action. This is the useful counterpart to the holographic spectacle.

Make the persona's silhouette strong before refining the decorative frame. Thor has storm-blue mass, armor and helmet; Loki has elegant yellow-gold horns/core with emerald secondary detail; Odin has a crown, beard and one luminous eye in warm gold. Preserve green Sylvie and existing persona duties. Supporting micro-agents remain substantially smaller. Do not reveal the secret realm.

Favor cinematic construction: a shaded inner mass, coherent facial planes, sparse structural lines, surface-sampled particles and carefully bounded glow. Keep the face readable against the background at normal laptop brightness. Do not let transparency, bloom, noise or scanlines flatten it into a luminous tangle. A wireframe-only head that looks unfinished requires geometric/material revision, not more particles.

Use local assets or licensed/provenanced development assets. No static screenshot can pass as the main renderer, and no modeling interface should be exposed to the user. Use the supplied concept art for composition and material intent, while creating original runtime geometry suitable for the device.
