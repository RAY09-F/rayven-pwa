These are product hypotheses for ASGARD, not installed revenue systems, guaranteed income or claims that a customer will buy. Validate actual demand and delivery effort before charging for an automated service. The local workbench can calculate user-supplied pricing and cost scenarios now. The build queue describes the missing integrations explicitly.

Do not invent prices from the concept art. Start with delivery time, verified provider costs, support burden and a customer interview. Use Target margin price, Acquisition cost, Payback, Automation time savings and AI usage cost scenario to compare assumptions. A model result is not actual profit.

## A coherent path from idea to recurring delivery

1. Choose one narrow offer with a verifiable output, such as a sourced research brief. Write its scope, turnaround, source requirements, limits and acceptance criteria.
2. Deliver a small manually reviewed pilot through existing authorized tools. Record actual time, costs, errors and customer feedback.
3. Add a customer record and approval queue with tenant boundaries. Do not reuse the founder's personal memory as shared customer memory.
4. Add verified checkout/subscription events, idempotent billing reconciliation and server-enforced entitlement. A decorative Subscribe button is not a payment integration.
5. Automate the repeatable read-only portions. Retain source links and an explicit editorial/customer approval step before public delivery or consequential action.
6. Add spending limits, cancellation, retry/dead-letter handling, run evidence and a pause switch before increasing volume.
7. Measure actual recurring revenue, churn, delivery cost and service failures. Compare them to the hypothesis and revise the offer.

This order is an engineering judgment based on the inspected project: it already has research and routine primitives, but this iteration does not establish production multi-tenancy, billing or customer lifecycle integration.

## Automation recipes to build around actual events

| Chain | Trigger | Read/prepare | Approval or boundary | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified inquiry → proposal | Submitted, consenting inquiry | Match fit, gather supplied scope, draft offer | Review exact quote and recipient | Accepted proposal or explicit decline |
| Paid research order → report | Verified payment event | Resolve entitlement, gather sources, draft report | Editorial review before delivery | Delivered report version and receipt |
| Subscription renewal → briefing | Verified active subscription and supported schedule | Read assigned sources and current priorities | Customer scope and budget | Sent approved artifact linked to sources |
| Failed payment → recovery | Actual billing failure | Gather invoice context and draft reminder | Consent and contact policy | Provider result; no inferred payment recovery |
| Meeting → follow-through | Authorized meeting summary | Extract explicit tasks and dates | Review owners and changes | Created task IDs from actual tool results |
| Incident → repair | Verified monitor failure | Gather logs, revision and reproduction | Approval before deployment | Passing checks and verified deployed revision |
| Content brief → publication | Approved editorial brief | Research, draft, source/license review | Explicit public publishing approval | Actual publication URL and timestamp |
| Customer cancellation → closure | Authenticated cancellation request | Stop future renewal/workflows, prepare export | Ownership and retention requirements | Provider cancellation and closure evidence |
| Weekly operations → review | Supported approved schedule | Read real usage, failures and commitments | No fabricated dashboard counts | Snapshot with data period and missing sources |
| Failed automation → recovery | Persisted failed run | Identify cause, prepare retry/compensation | No repeated consequential action without deduplication | Recorded replay or manual resolution |

## What is usable in this delivery
