=====================================================================
HOW TO USE THIS — READ THIS PART, SKIM THE REST
=====================================================================

  * DO NOT READ IT TOP TO BOTTOM. Nobody should. Search it. Ctrl+F a
    word — "clip", "sleep", "cable", "Loki", "$0", "Bakersfield" — and
    read what comes up.
  * Every entry has a number. When you want one built, say the number.
    "Codex, build 0412." "Claude, tell me more about 0088." That is the
    whole point of the numbers.
  * Every entry says what it costs. "$0" means zero. If you are cutting
    spend — and you are — filter on "$0" first. Most of this list is $0.
  * The gods do not run any business on their own and they never touch
    real money. Anything that would send, post, spend or trade ships
    disabled and waits for your "approve". That was true in the two big
    briefs I wrote you and it is true here.
  * Pick THREE. Not thirty. Three things, two weeks, then come back to
    the list. A list this size is a pantry, not a plate.
  * Anything marked "verify price" — check it the day you buy it. Prices
    and free tiers change monthly.
  * The two big briefs already written are referenced by name, not
    repeated: the ALWAYS-ON brief (wake words, clip that, the tray
    companion) and the ODIN HIGH SEAT brief (paper trading, the forge,
    the forty strategies, the forty businesses). This list adds to
    them; it does not restate them.
