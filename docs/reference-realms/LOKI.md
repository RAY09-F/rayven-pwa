The 2.4-unit crystal has 80 individually flat-normal triangles across five intentionally positioned octagonal tiers, with pointed ends, an asymmetric belt, amber faces and lemon highlights. The narrow raised luminous inclusion is a controlled opaque inner-core approximation, not physical transmission or ray-traced refraction. No reference images, texture planes, humanoids or external resources are used. Lit metal materials and concentric beveled rims form the polished dais; its reflections depend on scene lighting/environment, without a fabricated photograph reflection.
