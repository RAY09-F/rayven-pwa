# Odin reference reconstruction

Owned module: `public/ui/realm-odin.js`, exporting `createOdin(THREE, {quality, preview})` under `CONTRACT.md`.

The inspected reference has a cluster of narrow, differently sized gold vertical masses over a recessed concentric command foundation. Broad, dark slab paving and five radial connections establish architectural scale; low basalt perimeter walls and sparse trees provide depth. The implementation uses this hierarchy with seven bevel-extruded, pitched pylons, gold collars, independently modeled buttresses, a dark central basin, stepped metal/stone annuli, individually jointed pavement sectors, bridge decks, real ascending stairs, small parapets, basalt blocks and instanced conifers. The integration owns all five advisor gems and platforms.

Tower body height is approximately 2.44 units; tower top is 3.621. Foundation radius is 2.23, plaza radius 5.5 (bevel numerics extend its box by 0.00024). Bridges follow the five contract anchors and stairs finish at 0.634 against the integrated 0.65 platform. Main architecture remains fixed. `hero` contains only the movable tower cluster, its collars/braces and core; `update` never changes its transform. State-driven core/channel emphasis has a slow, small optional pulse; Still mode stops that pulse. No generated activity, listener, RAF, network, screenshot texture or global scene is used.

The original reference's photographed weathering, inscriptions, deep canyon vista and detailed sculptural ornaments are not reproduced pixel-for-pixel. Material depth comes from rough lit stone/metal, bevels, inset panels, slab joints and real side walls plus two original deterministic 128×128 procedural mineral/brushed-grain textures. These local DataTextures supply muted albedo variation, bump relief and roughness; they contain no reference imagery. GPU appearance must be judged in the integrated renderer by the lead.

`preview:true` omits outer plaza, bridges, walls and terrain while retaining the complete central monument. Low quality reduces terrain placements. Shared geometry is reused within the factory; no resources are shared between instances. Disposal releases unique geometries/materials and InstancedMesh instance resources and is idempotent.

Validation with local Three r185 via Node: every position component finite for full and preview factories; computed bounds finite; Arrange hero position preserved after updates; Still core intensity unchanged across timestamps; double disposal succeeds. Full: 185 mesh objects, 76 unique geometries, 12 materials, two textures, approximately 47,320 rendered triangles including instances. Preview: 35 meshes, 35 geometries, 12 materials, two textures, approximately 18,836 triangles. Full bounds approximately X/Z ±5.50, Y -0.25–3.621. Preview bounds X/Z ±2.23, Y 0.14–3.621. Browser/GPU validation belongs to integration and was not performed by this specialist.
