From proposal to paid work
  1. Choose one type of customer and inspect a real, publicly visible website problem.
  2. Confirm the buyer’s desired outcome and decision-maker. Do not promise unsupported integrations.
  3. Agree a written scope, delivery window, revision limit and payment terms.
  4. Build and test with sample data; obtain approval before changing the customer’s live system.
  5. Deliver a walkthrough and operating instructions. Record actual hours, costs, payment received and any follow-up request.
