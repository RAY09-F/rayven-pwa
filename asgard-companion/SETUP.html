Controls

Ctrl + Shift + M toggles listening. Names are ignored while muted. The Chrome panel is optional; the listener runs independently.

Chrome

Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select C:\Asgard\asgard-face. It is already paired. Enable the page face through its popup.
